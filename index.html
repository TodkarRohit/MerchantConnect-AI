The Problem: Traditional Merchant Sales

Why typical outreach pipelines bleed money & miss targets

  • • Slow human response times: Leads wait 4-24 hours for a sales rep call; by then 70% have signed up with competitors.
  • • Language & slang disconnect: Rigid English scripts fail with Kirana and retail merchants who speak conversational Hinglish.
  • • High sales rep fatigue: Reps waste 80% of their day cold-calling dead numbers instead of closing hot deals.
  • • Passive chatbots: Web chat widgets wait for visitors to ask FAQs; they never initiate proactive sales pipelines.
✅

The Solution: MerchantConnect AI Teammate

Autonomous, multi-turn, culturally adapted sales intelligence

  • • Proactive WhatsApp Outreach: Initiates personalized conversations the second a lead enters your database.
  • • Native Hinglish & Tone Adaptation: Understands Indian merchant colloquialisms, price sensitivity, and business challenges.
  • • Autonomous Qualification (BANT): Automatically scores leads into Hot, Warm, or Cold based on revenue, budget, and urgency.
  • • Human-in-the-Loop Escalation: Seamlessly transfers high-intent ready-to-buy merchants with full context to human closers.
